## How to Apply a Brand Design System

1. Identify the aesthetic or brand requested (e.g. "make it look like Linear" or "use Stripe design system").
2. Read the corresponding `DESIGN.md` file from `.agents/design-systems/design-md/<brand>/DESIGN.md`.
3. Extract and enforce:
   - **Color ladder & tints** (e.g. canvas `#010102`, surface ladders, precise hex accents).
   - **Typography scales, weights, and letter-spacings**.
   - **Border-radius hierarchy and hairline stroke treatments**.
   - **Component padding, interaction hover/focus states, and layout grids**.
